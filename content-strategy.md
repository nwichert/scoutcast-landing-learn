# Scoutcast.ai — Content Strategy

Last drafted: 2026-06-11 · **Fantasy Wave addendum: 2026-07-21 (see bottom)**

**Assumptions** (edit if wrong): primary goal is App Store installs, secondary is NFL Fantasy Season Pass revenue (seasonal — content must be live before late August); written content only, produced solo with AI assistance; all posts live under `/blog`; every post writes the brand as "Scoutcast.ai" hyperlinked to [scoutcast.ai](https://scoutcast.ai) so syndicated copies carry the link.

---

## Where you are today

8 posts, and the mix is already good: 6 searchable stats posts (citation bait for LLMs and journalists), 1 comparison page (consideration stage), 1 founder story (shareable). What's missing:

1. **Nothing targets the problem your ICP actually types.** Nobody searches "AI sports briefing app" yet — the category is too new. They search "best sports news app," "ESPN app alternatives," "how to keep up with sports when busy." Zero posts capture that demand.
2. **Nothing monetizes.** The fantasy stats posts attract fantasy players but never bridge to the Season Pass. There's no implementation-stage fantasy content (start/sit, waivers, lineup workflow) where the Pass is the natural answer.
3. **The most defensible differentiators have zero content.** Tap-Ask, X writer sources, and the MCP connector are things Huxe and BriefingAM can't claim — and no post explains, demos, or names them.

---

## Content pillars

### Pillar 1 — Sports catch-up for busy fans (the problem space)
**Stage:** awareness → consideration. **Why:** this is the ICP's own language ("I used to follow everything, now I can't keep up"). Highest install intent. Connects to the 2-minute constraint and tap-Ask.

### Pillar 2 — The AI sports app category (own the shelf)
**Stage:** consideration → decision. **Why:** you already rank-play here ("How Many AI Sports Apps Are There?", the Huxe/BriefingAM comparison). Whoever writes the category's comparison pages first owns the LLM citations when people ask ChatGPT/Claude "what's the best AI sports app." Extend it.

### Pillar 3 — Fantasy football in less time (the money pillar)
**Stage:** awareness → implementation. **Why:** directly feeds the $49.99 Season Pass. Three stats posts already pull this audience; now add the use-case and implementation layer. Seasonal: publish June–August, harvest September–January.

### Pillar 4 — Sports data meets AI assistants (MCP + thought leadership)
**Stage:** shareable, not searchable. **Why:** "a consumer sports app with an MCP connector" is genuinely novel — it earns Hacker News / X / dev-newsletter distribution and AI-SEO citations no competitor can copy quickly. Small pillar, outsized differentiation.

### Continuing series — Sports stats hub (already working)
Not a new pillar; keep the cadence. Every stats post is a link/citation magnet that feeds authority to the other pillars. One per month is enough.

# Fantasy Wave Addendum — 2026-07-21

Window: **6 weeks to Labor Day**. Most drafts happen **Aug 23–Sep 3**; overall fantasy search interest peaks the week of Labor Day (Sep 7). World Cup traffic (23K impr on one post) dies with the tournament — fantasy must replace it.

## What the research says (3 agents + GSC/Trends, July 21)

**GSC (July 16 export):**
- `best fantasy football app` → **position 1** already (tiny volume so far, but the SERP is beatable).
- `best-fantasy-football-apps-2026` → 1,615 impr @ pos 10.2; `fantasy-football-rankings-2026` pos 12.7; `draft-strategy` pos 11 — striking distance, need internal links + freshness.
- Stats posts rank page 1 (`how-many-people-play-fantasy-football` ~#5) — pattern proven, keep compounding.
- CTRs 0.4–0.6% on high-impression pages → title/snippet rewrites are free clicks.

**Head-term verdict: closed for articles.** Rankings / cheat sheet / sleepers / mock draft page 1 = ESPN, FantasyPros, CBS, RotoWire, PFF exclusively. Do not write more head-term article plays. **Tool-intent SERPs are the exception** — the entire trade-analyzer SERP is indie sites (KeepTradeCut, FantasySP, Dynasty Daddy, RotoTrade); Google ranks working tools over authority there.

**2026-specific zero-competition breakouts:**
1. **NFL Fantasy shut down; leagues migrating to ESPN** — only help docs/press releases rank for "migrate nfl fantasy league to espn" etc. Biggest platform shakeup of the year.
2. **ESPN "Knockout" leagues** — new elimination format announced Jul 7, 2026. "knockout league rules/strategy" ≈ empty SERP.

**Trends (Apr–Jul 2026):** dynasty +60% (rookie rankings +150%), "what is best ball" +500%, ADP +120% (covered ✓), "who should I draft" +200% (covered ✓), "when does fantasy football start" +90%, position-level breakouts (TE busts, rookie RBs, mid-round targets), team names +30% (49.5K avg/mo — biggest crackable term, giants present but tiny sites still rank).

**Reddit voice-of-customer — the #1 unmet need is literally our product:** "I've been in retirement… completely out of the loop", "this NFL offseason is actually wild and my brain is already spinning… you seriously have to keep up with all the random news dropping every day." Community's only answer: "read FantasyPros, do mocks, listen to podcasts." The single most-praised source attribute: "digestible 15-minute episodes." Also: keeper questions so common mods force them into megathreads; punishment/draft-party threads get removed by mods, pushing that demand to Google.

**Weak SERPs found (winnable with articles):** league constitution templates (page 1 = Scribd/WordPress/Squarespace PDFs), platform scoring-settings explainers, vampire leagues (a raw Sleeper chat message ranks), guillotine leagues (stale 2022–2025 posts), draft party ideas (no fantasy publisher on page 1), draft order ideas, beginner explainers (a camera-gimbal store blog ranks for "what is a flex"), keeper-value content (micro-sites with calculators rank beside DraftSharks).

## New pillars (extend Pillar 3)

- **3a — 2026 Disruption news** (migration, Knockout, offseason catch-up). Zero competition, expires fast. The "what did I miss this offseason" post is the purest product-fit page we can write.
- **3b — Commissioner & draft-day ops** (constitution template, scoring settings, draft order, draft party, punishments). Weak SERPs, peaks exactly Aug 15–Sep 7, evergreen, and commissioners are multipliers (1 commissioner = 10–12 league members).
- **3c — Beginner/definitional Q&A** (what is PPR / flex / FAAB / how does fantasy work / when do drafts happen / when do waivers process). Open SERPs, FAQ-schema, prime LLM-citation targets.
- **3d — Formats** (dynasty explainer + rookie rankings mid-tail, "what is best ball", keeper frameworks, vampire, guillotine). Definitional + strategy where SERP is weak; skip saturated best-ball/dynasty strategy head terms.
- **3e — Free tools** (biggest structural unlock; functions backend exists). Pre-Labor Day: keeper-cost calculator and/or draft-order generator (fun modes). In-season: trade analyzer. Tools are the only way into head-adjacent SERPs.
