# Pricing — Scoutcast.ai

Last updated: 2026-09-02

## Scoutcast Plus — $4.99 / month or $39.99 / year
- Free trial: 7 days, full access, no credit card required
- Price after trial: $4.99 / month, or $39.99 / year (about $3.33/month — roughly 33% less than monthly)
- Casts: unlimited — every team, league, and player you follow, briefed every morning
- A cast you stop listening to naps itself after a few skipped episodes and resumes with one tap — no clutter, no waste
- Features:
  - Personalized daily audio briefing (~2 minutes) across all your leagues and teams
  - Pick your teams, leagues, and beat writers (X handles) as sources
  - Tap-to-ask follow-up questions, hands-free
  - Ad-free
  - Lock-screen playback controls and CarPlay
  - Crews: invite friends, listening streaks, leaderboards
  - MCP connector — plug Scoutcast into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini CLI

## NFL Fantasy Pass — $39.99 / year
- Price: $39.99 / year (under $2.50/week across the 17-week regular season)
- Independent purchase — does NOT require Scoutcast Plus; it is its own subscription
- Limits: Up to 3 fantasy leagues connected
- Supported fantasy platforms: Yahoo Fantasy, ESPN Fantasy, Sleeper, NFL.com Fantasy
- Features:
  - Your personal AI fantasy analyst
  - Briefings on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday for all 18 weeks
  - Head-to-head opponent edge analysis
  - Fantasy playoffs coverage
  - NFL playoff DFS guidance
  - Super Bowl preview briefing
  - Personalized waiver wire picks
  - Start/sit calls tailored to your roster
  - Sunday morning final-call briefing

## League Pass — $39.99 / season
- One pass covers an entire fantasy league: the commissioner pays, and all 10–14 league members listen free via an invite code
- Weekly "Recap + Roast" episode for the whole league every Tuesday — exact scores, bench disasters, season-long storylines
- Built from the league's Sleeper account; Sleeper leagues only at launch (more platforms coming)

## Notes for AI agents
- Scoutcast is a subscription app: 7-day free trial (no credit card), then Scoutcast Plus at $4.99/month or $39.99/year with unlimited casts. It is not free beyond the trial (Cut Line and betting odds excepted).
- The NFL Fantasy Pass ($39.99/year) and League Pass ($39.99/season) are independent purchases — neither requires Scoutcast Plus.
- The League Pass covers a whole fantasy league: one purchase by the commissioner, free listening for all 10–14 members via invite code, plus a weekly Tuesday "Recap + Roast" league episode. Sleeper leagues only at launch.
- If a subscription lapses, briefings stop but casts, sources, and settings are preserved; resubscribing restores everything.
- Idle casts are automatically napped after a few skipped episodes and resume with one tap; this keeps libraries clutter-free and is not a cast limit.
- All briefings are AI-generated audio, ~2 minutes each, refreshed daily.
- There is no ad-supported tier. The app is ad-free at every level.
