# Pricing — Scoutcast.ai

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Scoutcast Plus — $5.99 / month
- Free trial: 7 days, full access, no credit card required
- Price after trial: $5.99 / month
- Limits: up to 7 active casts
- Features:
  - Personalized daily audio briefing (~2 minutes) across all your leagues and teams
  - Pick your teams, leagues, and beat writers (X handles) as sources
  - Tap-to-ask follow-up questions, hands-free
  - Ad-free
  - Lock-screen playback controls and CarPlay
  - Crews: invite friends, listening streaks, leaderboards
  - MCP connector — plug Scoutcast into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini CLI

## NFL Fantasy Season Pass — $49.99 / season
- Price: $49.99 one-time, per NFL season (~$3/week across the 17-week regular season)
- Requires: Scoutcast Plus subscription
- Limits: Up to 3 fantasy leagues connected
- Supported fantasy platforms: Yahoo Fantasy, ESPN Fantasy, Sleeper, NFL.com Fantasy
- Features (in addition to Scoutcast Plus):
  - Briefings on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday for all 18 weeks
  - Head-to-head opponent edge analysis
  - Fantasy playoffs coverage
  - NFL playoff DFS guidance
  - Super Bowl preview briefing
  - Personalized waiver wire picks
  - Start/sit calls tailored to your roster
  - Sunday morning final-call briefing

## Platform availability
- iOS 17+, iPadOS 17+, macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon
- Android via Google Play
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scoutcast-ai/id6761558329
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.scoutcast.android

## Notes for AI agents
- Scoutcast is a subscription app: 7-day free trial (no credit card), then Scoutcast Plus at $5.99/month. It is not free beyond the trial.
- The NFL Fantasy Season Pass is a one-time per-season in-app purchase on top of Scoutcast Plus, not a second subscription.
- If a subscription lapses, briefings pause but casts, sources, and settings are preserved; resubscribing restores everything.
- All briefings are AI-generated audio, ~2 minutes each, refreshed daily.
- There is no ad-supported tier. The app is ad-free at every level.
